01 — THE AGENT

Not a chatbot.
A crew member with a memory.

Dedalus runs on a VPS the team owns, around the clock. When a member asks at 2 a.m., the answer is already researched. When the Notion board drifts, it gets repaired. When a decision is made on a call, it is written down — with a timestamp — before anyone forgets.

The naming is the mission statement: in the myth, Daedalus built the wings that let Icarus fly. This Daedalus exists to hand team ICARUS everything it needs to fly — tools, knowledge, structure — and to say “no” loudly before anything irreversible happens without a human's sign-off.

“Nothing important should live only in one person's head — or one chat thread.”
